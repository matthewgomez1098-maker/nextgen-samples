Signs it's time for new floors

  • Boards that squeak, bounce or feel soft when you walk across them
  • Gaps between hardwood planks that stay open all year
  • Cracked or hollow-sounding tiles, or grout that keeps crumbling
  • Vinyl or laminate that's peeling, lifting or swelling at the seams
  • Carpet with matted traffic lanes, stains that come back, or lingering pet odor
  • Water damage near the dishwasher, fridge, toilet or tub
  • Worn finish where you can see bare wood in the busiest paths
  • You're selling soon and the floors look dated in listing photos
