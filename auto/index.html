Good to know

Signs your car needs a visit

  • The check engine light is on, or it's flashing
  • Squealing or grinding when you brake
  • The steering wheel shakes at freeway speed
  • The car pulls to one side on a straight road
  • The AC blows warm or smells musty
  • A puddle or spots under the car after it sits overnight
  • The engine cranks slowly on cold mornings
  • A chip or crack in the windshield that's starting to spread

Questions

Answers before you call

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments get you in fastest, but we take walk-ins all day.

Do you work with my insurance?

Yes. We work with every major insurer on collision and glass claims.

Will you call before doing extra work?

Always. Nothing is done without your approval.

Is there a warranty?

24 months or 24,000 miles on parts and labor.

How long does a diagnostic take?

Most are done within two to three hours. Intermittent problems that come and go can take longer, and we'll tell you before going past the first hour.

Can I bring my own parts?

We can install customer parts in some cases, but the 24/24 warranty only covers parts we supply.

Will using your shop affect my factory warranty?

Routine maintenance can be done at any qualified shop. Keep your receipts, and we keep a copy of every service on file too.

Do you give loaner cars?

We offer a free shuttle within 5 miles. For longer repairs, we can help set up a rental.
