About us

A full-service shop in Chino that shows its work

Torque & Tread is a repair, tire, collision and glass shop in one building in Chino. That means one drop-off and one invoice when your car needs more than one kind of work. A fender bender that cracked the windshield and knocked the alignment out gets fixed by people who talk to each other every day.

Our ASE-certified techs work on domestic and import cars, trucks and SUVs. Every car that comes in gets a digital inspection. You get photos and notes by text, each item marked urgent, soon or fine, with a price next to it. You approve what you want done and skip the rest.

We back our repairs with a 24-month / 24,000-mile warranty on parts and labor. We keep old parts for you to see if you ask. If you live or work within 5 miles, our shuttle can take you there and bring you back when the car is ready.

Signs your car needs a visit

  • The check engine light is on, or it's flashing
  • Squealing or grinding when you brake
  • The steering wheel shakes at freeway speed
  • The car pulls to one side on a straight road
  • The AC blows warm or smells musty
  • A puddle or spots under the car after it sits overnight
  • The engine cranks slowly on cold mornings
  • A chip or crack in the windshield that's starting to spread
