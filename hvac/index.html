About us

Local heating and air from Rancho Cucamonga

Coldfront Heating & Air repairs, replaces and maintains air conditioners, heat pumps, furnaces and ductless systems across the Inland Empire. Our trucks run out of Rancho Cucamonga and carry the parts that fail most often in summer, so most repairs get done on the first visit.

Every call starts the same way. You pick a two-hour arrival window, and the tech texts you when they're on the way. They find the problem, show you what they found, and give you a flat price before any work starts. If you go ahead with the repair, the diagnostic fee is waived.

We'd rather fix your system than sell you a new one. When a repair makes sense, we say so. When a system is old and needs a major part, we show you the repair price and the replacement price side by side and let you decide.

Signs your AC needs service

  • Warm or barely cool air coming from the vents.
  • Ice on the copper line by the outdoor unit or on the indoor coil.
  • The outdoor unit hums but the fan doesn't spin.
  • The system turns on and off every few minutes.
  • Water around the furnace or indoor unit, or no water dripping from the outside drain line.
  • New noises like buzzing, grinding, screeching or banging.
  • One or two rooms are much hotter than the rest of the house.
  • Your summer power bill jumped and your habits didn't change.
