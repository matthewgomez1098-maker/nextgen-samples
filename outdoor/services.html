Weekly pool service

Your tech comes on the same day each week and follows the same routine every visit. We test the water on site, balance it, and leave a short service note so you know what was done and what we found.

Summer heat, heavy swim weekends and Santa Ana winds all change what your pool needs. We adjust chemicals visit by visit and let you know if we see a problem developing.

  • Skim the surface and net the bottom
  • Brush walls, steps and tile line
  • Vacuum as needed
  • Empty skimmer and pump baskets
  • Test and balance chlorine, pH, alkalinity and stabilizer
  • Check pump, filter pressure, heater and timer
  • Monthly check of calcium hardness and salt level

Equipment repair and green pool recovery

We repair and replace pumps, motors, filters, heaters, salt systems, automation and lighting. Variable-speed pumps run quietly at low speed for most of the day and can cut pump energy use significantly. We'll quote one side by side with a repair if your pump is near the end of its life.

Green pools happen after a broken pump, a missed week in summer or a long vacation. A recovery starts with a heavy shock and filter clean, then daily visits to brush, vacuum and re-test until the water is clear and balanced. Most pools are swimmable in three to five days. Pools that have been green for months may need to be drained first, and we'll tell you after the first visit.

Tree trimming, removal and palms

Our certified arborist sets the plan for every tree job. Good pruning thins a canopy so wind passes through, lifts branches off the roof, and keeps the tree healthy. We don't top trees or strip them bare, because both leave weak regrowth that breaks in the next windstorm.

Palms need different care. We remove dead and hanging fronds, seed pods and fruit stalks, and we can skin the trunk for a clean look. Leaving the green fronds in place keeps the palm strong. Plan palm trimming once a year, ideally after flowering in summer.

For removals, we take down the tree in sections, haul it away and grind the stump if you'd like. Some cities protect certain trees by species or size, so we check what your city requires before any removal. In spring we look for active bird nests before cutting and adjust the timing if we find one.

Drought-tolerant design and irrigation

A good drought-tolerant yard looks full and green for most of the year. We design with native and Mediterranean plants like sages, manzanita, salvias, ornamental grasses, agaves and olive trees, grouped by how much water they need. Decomposed granite paths, boulders and dry creek beds add structure and handle winter rain.

Irrigation is where most of the water savings come from. We convert spray heads to drip lines and low-flow rotators, add a smart controller that adjusts to the weather, and set separate zones for trees, shrubs and any lawn you keep. If your yard qualifies for a local water district turf replacement program, we take the before photos and handle the paperwork. We check what's being offered when we quote.

  • Lawn removal and soil preparation
  • Planting plan grouped by water need
  • Drip and low-flow conversion
  • Weather-based smart controller
  • Mulch or decomposed granite top layer

Sod, pavers and hardscape

If you want lawn for kids and dogs, we'll give you a healthy one. We remove the old grass, grade and amend the soil, check sprinkler coverage, and lay fresh sod that's rooted within about two weeks. Warm-season grasses handle our summers with far less water than fescue.

Our paver patios, walkways and driveways sit on a compacted gravel base with edge restraints and polymeric sand in the joints, so they stay flat and weed-free. We also build seat walls, fire pit surrounds, steps and outdoor kitchen pads.

Landscape lighting

Lighting makes a yard usable after sunset and makes the house look its best from the street. We use low-voltage LED fixtures: path lights along walkways, uplights on trees and walls, step lights for safety, and soft string or wall lights around patios.

Every system runs on a transformer with a timer or photocell, and most can be controlled from your phone. We plan wire runs during the build so nothing has to be dug up later. A typical front and back yard system runs $3,500 to $9,000 depending on fixture count.

Questions about services

Do I need to be home for weekly pool service?

No. We just need gate access and a clear path to the equipment. You'll get a service note after every visit.

Will you trim a tree that's near power lines?

Branches near power lines are handled by the utility's line-clearance crews. We'll tell you if that applies and help you contact them.

How much water does a drought-tolerant yard save?

It depends on the size of your lawn and how you water now. Most clients see a clear drop in summer bills once the plants are established.

Can you work with my existing sprinklers?

Often, yes. We'll test every zone and tell you which parts can stay and which should be converted to drip.
