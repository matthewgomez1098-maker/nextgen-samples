About us

One crew for the pool, the trees and everything in between

Canopy & Cove started on a single pool route in San Dimas. Customers kept asking if we knew someone for their trees, then their sprinklers, then a patio. Today we care for pools, trees and landscapes across the San Gabriel Valley, and we still run every job with our own crew.

Having one team on your property makes a real difference. The pool tech notices a sprinkler overspraying the deck. The arborist plans a trim so leaves stop landing in the water. Your design accounts for the pool equipment, the shade line and the way you actually use the yard.

We design for the way Southern California works now: less lawn, smarter irrigation, plants that handle August heat, and outdoor rooms you can use most of the year. A certified arborist on staff oversees all of our tree work.

Time to call us when you notice

  • Cloudy, green or slippery pool water
  • A pump that hums, rattles or won't prime
  • Branches resting on the roof or rubbing the house
  • Brown, hanging palm fronds and seed pods
  • Lawn with dry patches no matter how much you water
  • Sprinklers spraying the sidewalk or running off into the gutter
  • Pavers that are sinking, shifting or growing weeds
  • A water bill that climbs every summer
