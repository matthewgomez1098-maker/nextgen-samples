Foothill front yard, Glendora

The problem: A thirsty lawn on a sunny slope, with brown patches every summer and runoff down the driveway every time the sprinklers ran.

What we did: Removed the lawn, regraded the slope with a dry creek bed to slow winter rain, and planted sages, manzanita, deer grass and a multi-trunk olive. Converted every spray zone to drip and added a weather-based controller. We handled the turf program photos and paperwork.

  • Timeline: 2.5 weeks
  • Size: about 1,400 sq ft
Sample budget range$22,000 to $30,000

Green to clear, San Dimas

The problem: A pool left alone for most of a summer after the pump motor failed. The water was dark green and the filter cartridges were clogged solid.

What we did: Replaced the failed pump with a variable-speed model, replaced the cartridges, and ran a full green-pool recovery with daily visits. The pool was swimmable on day four. The family stayed on weekly service and the water has stayed clear.

  • Timeline: 4 days
  • Ongoing: weekly service
Sample budget range$2,100 to $2,900

Patio, fire pit and lighting, La Verne

The problem: A cracked concrete slab and a backyard nobody used after dark.

What we did: Broke out and hauled the old slab, then built a 600 sq ft paver patio with a seat wall and a built-in gas fire pit. Added path lights, tree uplights and wall lights on a phone-controlled transformer. Planted a border of grasses and lavender on drip.

  • Timeline: 3 weeks
  • Permits: gas line, handled by us

Oak care before wind season, Claremont

The problem: Two mature oaks with heavy limbs over the roof and a third with dead wood in the crown, ahead of fall Santa Ana winds.

What we did: Our certified arborist assessed all three trees and wrote a pruning plan. The crew removed deadwood, reduced the limbs over the roof and raised the canopy off the house, keeping each tree's natural shape. We checked city requirements for oak work before starting.

  • Timeline: 2 days
  • Cleanup: full haul-away and blow-off
Sample budget range$3,200 to $4,800

Sod and paver refresh, Covina

The problem: A patchy backyard lawn the kids and dog had worn down to dirt, with a sunken flagstone path to the side gate.

What we did: Removed the old lawn, amended the soil, fixed sprinkler coverage, and laid warm-season sod. Replaced the flagstone with a paver walkway on a compacted base so it stays level.

  • Timeline: 1 week
  • Size: 900 sq ft sod, 120 sq ft pavers
Sample budget range$9,500 to $13,500

Full backyard with pool care, Diamond Bar

The problem: A dated backyard around a good pool. Old shrubs, a leaking sprinkler system, worn coping and palms dropping fronds into the water.

What we did: Trimmed and cleaned four palms, cleared the overgrown beds, and replanted with low-water shrubs and grasses on drip. Built a new paver deck around the pool, added step and uplighting, and moved the pool onto our weekly route.

  • Timeline: 4 weeks
  • Ongoing: weekly pool and seasonal care
