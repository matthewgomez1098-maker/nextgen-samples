Good to know

Call us when you notice

  • A water bill that jumps with no change in how you use water
  • Hot water that runs out faster than it used to
  • Slow drains in more than one sink, tub or shower
  • A garage door that's loud, jerky or lifts unevenly
  • A gap in the spring coil above your garage door
  • Ant trails along baseboards, counters or window sills

Questions

Answers before you call

Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates are free for installs and replacements. Repairs have a small diagnostic fee, waived with the repair.

Are your techs background-checked?

Yes, every tech and cleaner.

Do you offer plans?

Yes, quarterly pest control and recurring cleaning plans at a lower rate.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us and we come back to make it right at no charge.

Do you charge to come out?

There's a small diagnostic fee for repairs. It's waived when you go ahead with the work. Estimates for water heaters and repipes are free.

How long does a water heater replacement take?

Most tank replacements take three to four hours. Tankless installs usually take most of a day, depending on venting and gas line work.

Do I need to be home?

Yes, or have an adult over 18 there to let us in and approve the price before work starts.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Our workmanship is guaranteed for 90 days, and new water heaters carry the manufacturer's warranty of up to 12 years.
