About us

One Fontana team for the four jobs every house needs

Most homeowners need a plumber, a garage door tech, a pest pro and a house cleaner at some point every year. Finding four good companies takes time, and keeping track of them takes more. We put all four under one roof in Fontana, with one phone number, one booking calendar and one set of standards.

Every tech and cleaner on our team is background-checked before their first visit. They arrive in a marked vehicle, wear shoe covers inside, and give you a written price before any work starts. If we miss your two-hour arrival window, you get $50 off. That promise is printed on every invoice.

We work across the Inland Empire, from Ontario and Rancho Cucamonga to Redlands and Moreno Valley. Our repairs carry a 90-day workmanship guarantee. If something we fixed isn't right, call us and we come back to make it right at no charge.
