Common questions

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks for most weekends, and six or more for spring and summer Saturdays.

Do you set up on grass and concrete?

Yes. We stake down on grass and use sandbags on concrete, driveways and turf.

What if it rains or gets windy?

If it's unsafe to run inflatables, we move your date or refund the inflatable portion.

Is there a deposit?

A small deposit holds your date. The rest is due on delivery day.
