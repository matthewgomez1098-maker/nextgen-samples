Booking and deposit

Check your date online or call us, pick your rentals, and we'll send one quote with delivery, setup and pickup included. A deposit of 20 percent holds your date and your rentals. The balance is due on delivery day by card or cash.

Need to add something? You can add items up to 48 hours before your party, as long as they're available. Spring and summer Saturdays book up first, so reserve six weeks or more ahead for those dates.

  • Cancel 7 or more days before your party for a full deposit refund.
  • Cancel inside 7 days and your deposit becomes a credit for any date in the next 12 months.
  • Weather cancellations follow our weather policy below.

Delivery windows and fees

We set up at least two hours before your start time. The day before your party, we text you a two-hour arrival window. On delivery day you get a text when the truck is on the way and another when setup is done.

Tents, tables and chairs for morning parties can be set up the evening before at no extra charge. Delivery is free on orders over $250. Smaller orders pay a flat delivery fee based on your city.

Getting your space ready

A little prep makes setup fast and safe. Our crew will look over the spot when we arrive. If it isn't safe for an inflatable or tent, we'll help you find a better spot in the yard.

  • Flat, level ground. We can't set inflatables on a slope or hill.
  • Clear the area of rocks, sticks, toys and pet waste. Mow the grass if it's long.
  • Turn off sprinklers 24 to 48 hours before delivery.
  • Let us know about sprinkler lines, pipes or cables under the setup spot.
  • One outdoor outlet within about 50 feet for each blower, ideally on its own circuit.
  • A gate or path at least 4 feet wide from the street to the setup spot.
  • Clear a parking spot in front of the house for the truck if you can.

Weather and wind policy

Inflatables can't be used in rain, lightning or strong wind. Our rule is to get everyone off and turn the blower off when winds reach about 15 miles per hour or gusts start lifting the unit. Tents stay up in light rain and are great for keeping guests dry.

We watch the forecast with you. If it looks unsafe the day before or the morning of your party, call us. You can move your date at no charge, or we'll refund the inflatable portion of your order. Tents, tables and chairs can still be delivered if you want them.

  • On hot days, set inflatables in the shade if you can, or pick a water slide.
  • If the weather turns during the party, unplug the blower once everyone is off and let it deflate.

Pickup

We pick up the same evening, usually between 7 and 9 pm. Parties that run late can switch to next-morning pickup. We'll text you a pickup window the day of.

  • Leave everything where we set it up. No need to fold or stack.
  • Keep the blower plugged in and running until our crew arrives.
  • Shake food and crumbs off linens and bring them inside if it might rain. Please don't wash them. We launder every linen ourselves.
  • Keep pets away from inflatables and tents until pickup.

Damage and cleaning policy

Normal wear and tear is on us. Grass stains, a little dirt and everyday spills are expected. Extra charges only apply when something needs special cleaning or can't be rented again.

We'll send photos of any damage before we charge for it.

  • Silly string, paint, glitter, slime or confetti inside an inflatable.
  • Holes or cuts from shoes, toys, pets or sharp objects.
  • Candle wax, permanent marker or burns on linens.
  • Broken or missing chairs, tables or tent parts.

Questions about how it works

Do I need to be home for delivery?

Someone 18 or older needs to be there to show us the setup spot and go over the safety rules. Pickup can happen without you if everything is easy to reach.

Can you set up at a park?

Yes. Many city parks need a permit for tents or inflatables, and some don't allow stakes. Check with the city first, and we'll bring sandbags if needed.

What if my yard isn't flat?

Send us a photo when you book. We'll tell you which units fit, or suggest a tent and tables instead.

Can you set up indoors?

Many of our bouncers fit in a gym or hall with a ceiling at least 15 feet high. We'll need the room's measurements first.

How do I pay the balance?

By card or cash on delivery day. We'll text you a receipt once setup is done.
