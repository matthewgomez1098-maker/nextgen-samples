Good to know

Before you book, check these

  • Measure your flattest open area. Most bounce houses need about 17×17 feet, and combo units need closer to 20×25.
  • Find the nearest outdoor outlet. Each blower needs one within about 50 feet, ideally on a circuit nothing else is using.
  • Measure your side gate. Rolled inflatables and tent parts come in on a dolly and need a gate at least 4 feet wide.
  • Count your guests and plan one chair per person, plus a few extra for late arrivals.
  • Look for sprinkler heads, pipes or cables where a tent or bouncer will go. We stake into the ground on grass.
  • Turn sprinklers off 24 to 48 hours before delivery so the ground is firm and dry.
  • Check with your HOA or city if the party is at a park or in a shared space. Some require a permit for tents or inflatables.
  • Think about shade. Afternoon sun in the Inland Empire is strong, and a tent or water slide keeps kids comfortable.

Questions

Answers before you call

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks for most weekends, and six or more for spring and summer Saturdays.

Do you set up on grass and concrete?

Yes. We stake down on grass and use sandbags on concrete, driveways and turf.

What if it rains or gets windy?

If it's unsafe to run inflatables, we move your date or refund the inflatable portion.

Is there a deposit?

A small deposit holds your date. The rest is due on delivery day.

Can adults use the bounce houses?

Our castle bouncer is built for kids. The combo units and water slide can handle teens and adults, within the rider limits printed on the unit.

How many tables and chairs do I need for 50 guests?

About seven 60-inch rounds or seven 6-foot banquet tables, plus 55 chairs. Add a table or two for food, gifts and the cake.

How much water does a water slide use?

The slide runs off a garden hose at a low trickle. Most of the water stays in the splash pool, and we drain it before pickup.

Can I keep the bounce house overnight?

Yes, for overnight pickup. It has to stay deflated and unplugged after dark, and an overnight rate applies.
