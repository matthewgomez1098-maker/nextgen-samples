What affects the price

Two cars with the same package can land at different prices. We quote your car after a quick look, and the quote covers every part and every hour.

  • Your car: premium factory systems and newer cars often need extra interface modules
  • Gear level: higher-end speakers, subs and amps
  • Fabrication: custom boxes, pillars, racks and finish work
  • Deadening: doors only, or doors, trunk, floor and roof
  • Power: larger systems may need upgraded wiring or a second battery

Warranty and financing

Every install is covered by our lifetime install warranty for as long as you own the car. If a connection, mount or wire we installed fails, we fix it at no charge. Gear we supply also carries its manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the claim for you.

Financing is available on approved credit, so you can build the system you want now and pay over time. Ask in the shop for current terms. You can also build in stages and add to your system as your budget allows.

Questions about packages

Is the price on the package final?

Package prices are a starting point. Your written quote lists every part and the labor for your car before we start.

Do I need a deposit?

A deposit holds your install date and orders any parts we don't stock. The balance is due at pickup.

Can I upgrade from Stage 1 to Stage 2 later?

Yes. We plan Stage 1 installs so the amp and sub can be added later without redoing work.

What does the lifetime install warranty cover?

Our workmanship: wiring, connections, mounts and fabrication, for as long as you own the car. Gear is covered by its manufacturer's warranty.
