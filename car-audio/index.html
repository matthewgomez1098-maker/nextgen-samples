About us

A small Azusa shop built around the install

Bassline is a custom car audio and electronics shop in Azusa. We design and install sound systems, subwoofer builds, CarPlay head units, cameras, lighting and tint for drivers across the San Gabriel Valley.

Great gear in a rushed install sounds thin, buzzes and rattles. We spend our time where it counts. Every connection is soldered and heat-shrunk. Every wire run is sleeved, routed away from sharp edges and hidden behind factory panels. Doors get deadened before speakers go in. When the hardware is done, we tune the system in your car with a microphone and a meter.

Start with a sound demo in one of our shop cars. Hear what a front stage, a sealed sub and a full system sound like, then plan a build around your budget. Every install is backed by our lifetime install warranty.

Signs your system is ready for an upgrade

  • Vocals sound muffled or seem to come from your knees
  • The music distorts when you turn it past halfway
  • Door panels buzz or rattle on bass notes
  • Your bass sounds boomy on some songs and disappears on others
  • Your headlights dim on heavy bass hits
  • Your phone connects over a cable or an aux cord with no CarPlay or Android Auto
  • You have no backup camera or the factory one is grainy at night
