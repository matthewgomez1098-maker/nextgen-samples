FAQ

Will this void my car's warranty?

No. We use harness adapters and never cut factory wiring.

How long does an install take?

Most systems are done in a day. Custom fabrication takes two to three.

Can I bring my own gear?

Yes, though our lifetime warranty covers gear we supply.

Can I hear it first?

Yes. Book a demo and listen in our shop cars.
